Competitive advantages over the manual workaround

1 · One record, not three

One tap replaces text, paper tick and photo retakes → single source of truth.

2 · Real-time, not relayed

Delivery updates dispatch directly → no texts to read and re-key.

3 · Data that protects the contract

Every stop feeds admin reporting → the reason enterprise customers buy and renew.
