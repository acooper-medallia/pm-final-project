Future-State Journey: Frontline Delivery Driver

RouteLogic Velocity (B2B) · From doorstep workaround to one trusted record

Future state · concept, not yet validated

Persona

Frontline delivery driver, 30–60+ stops a day, often one-handed and on weak signal.

Goal

Close every stop quickly, with each delivery recorded and proven as it happens.

Strategy

Reclaim operational simplicity: strip away legacy noise so the tool is the fastest way to do everyday work.

1

Start Route

At the depot

User action

Taps "Start Route" on a minimal home screen → the route loads instantly.

Internal state

Sees the stop list cached for offline use → leaves without screenshots or a paper manifest.

Pain point addressed

Buried core action and blank offline route → no backups needed.

BUG-2079 · BUG-2050 · UXR-06, UXR-11
2

Arrive at Stop

Pulling up to the address

User action

Opens a stop card showing only the address, access note and package → everything needed at a glance.

Internal state

Sees only what matters → feels focused, not overwhelmed by menus.

Pain point addressed

Menus inside menus moved behind "More" → no hunting for features.

UXR-04 · UXR-08 · UXR-11
3

Close Out Stop

At the doorstep · today's moment of misery

User action

One tap on "Delivered" and one photo on a single screen → the stop closes in seconds.

Internal state

Sees a "✓ Saved, will upload" badge → knows the delivery is recorded.

Pain point addressed

3-tap flow and silent photo failures → no retakes, no guessing.

BUG-2055 · BUG-2061 · UXR-01, UXR-07
Core frustration resolved
4

Confirmed & Synced

Driving to the next stop

User action

Drives on with nothing to text → dispatch already sees "Delivered" in real time.

Internal state

Trusts the app as the record → drops texts, WhatsApp and paper.

Pain point addressed

Dashboard lag and duplicate confirmations → one trusted source of truth.

BUG-2072 · UXR-02, UXR-09, UXR-12

Today: manual workaround

3 screens, 2–3 photo retakes, a text to dispatch and a tick on paper. The record ends up split across 4 places, and none of them is trusted.

Future state

1 tap and 1 photo, with visible confirmation and automatic retry. One record, visible to dispatch in real time.

1 · Faster per stop

One screen replaces 3 screens, photo retakes, a text and paper, saving time across every stop.

2 · Proof you can rely on

Confirmed, automatically retried uploads replace photos that may never have uploaded.

3 · Trusted enterprise data

Every delivery feeds reporting directly, protecting the value behind renewal.
