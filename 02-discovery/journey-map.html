Current-State Journey Map: Last-Mile Delivery Driver

RouteLogic Velocity (B2B) · Built from 12 research sessions (UXR-01 to UXR-12) and 10 bug reports (BUG-2031 to BUG-2090)

Role

Frontline driver running a 30–60+ stop route on Android, often one-handed and in low-signal areas. Uses about 5% of the product, all day long.

Goal

Finish every stop quickly, with each delivery recorded and proven the moment it happens, so dispatch never has to chase them.

Moment of misery

Closing out a stop at the doorstep: 3 taps across 3 screens, then a POD photo that may silently fail. The driver can't tell whether it worked.

Emotion

Core frustration: the doorstep close-out

The driver has done everything the app asked and still doesn't know whether the delivery was recorded. That uncertainty is what triggers the retakes, the texts to dispatch, and the paper backups. From that point on, the app duplicates the real system instead of being it.

"Proof-of-delivery photos take forever to upload and half fail. Drivers don't know if it worked, so they retake three times." (UXR-07, warehouse lead)
5 of 7 drivers keep a paper manifest "just in case the app dies"; 7 of 7 rank speed of core actions above any new feature. (UXR-12, driver focus group)
