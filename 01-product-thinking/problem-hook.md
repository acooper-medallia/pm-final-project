# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** RouteLogic
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If we do nothing for 12 months, the company will likely see their enterprise clients going into the marketplace looking for a leaner competitor.  As this continues and churn begins this will show that there is a leaner tool in the marketplace that can switch a client.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** The user is currently trying to bridge the gap using outside AI solutions on their own and reaching out to competitors to explore other options. Likely making decisions to use workarounds possibly causing information not matching across systems and inflating the amount of work the user has to do (if they do it at all).
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must stop our enterprise customers from churning and to leaner competitors by giving the users a faster, more focused workflow to achieve real time coordination while maintaining all features, but creating a more streamlined path available.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For the 95% of regional fleet dispatchers using RouteLogic daily, we will streamline core real-time route coordination by showing only essential information in the moment, moving the non-essential features behind necessary walls. This addresses immediate churn risks from our largest account trialing a competitor and protects the underlying data quality that powers enterprise features.
